      Reload from the original file?

      Faithful mode writes your edits back into the original package, so it has to start from the file as it was opened.

      The changes you made in Free mode will be discarded.

      Docx Studio

      Faithful — your edits are patched into the original document.xml, and every paragraph and run keeps the properties it was opened with. Styles, numbering, headers, footers, theme, settings and images are rewritten byte for byte. Use it on documents that must come back looking exactly as they went in.
      Free — the whole document is rebuilt from what you see, with its own styles and numbering. You get the full ribbon, including fonts, colours, tables and pictures. Formatting the reader does not support may normalise on save.
